Untitled Norse JRPG

A story-driven JRPG inspired by Norse mythology. Lead a party of three heroes through frozen villages, ancient ruins and raven-haunted cliffs in a world where the old gods are watching.

Combat is turn-based with an action point system, a visible turn order and finishing-blow bonuses. Characters grow through runes that are equipped using a limited Resonance budget or carved permanently into weapons at the forge, opening up different builds for every party member.

About me

Henrik Svedsäter

I'm a software engineer from Sweden with a BSc in Computer Science from Örebro University. Today I build software for camera- and laser-based measurement systems at Car-O-Liner.

I've been building things for the web since I was a kid, and over the years that turned into freelance work, a degree and a career in software development. I enjoy the whole stack, from database design to the small details in a user interface.

In my spare time I'm developing a JRPG inspired by Norse mythology in Unity, where I get to combine programming, 3D, game design and storytelling.
