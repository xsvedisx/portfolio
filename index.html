About me

Henrik Svedsäter

Originally from Stockholm, I moved to Örebro for love. Today we live in a cosy neighbourhood ten minutes outside the city, together with our two boys.

I'm a software engineer with a BSc in Computer Science from Örebro University. Today I build software for camera- and laser-based measurement systems at Car-O-Liner.

Ever since I was a kid I've been building things for the web, games and anything fun that crossed my mind. Over the years that turned into occasional freelance work, a degree and a career in software development. I enjoy the whole stack, from database design to the small details in a user interface.

In my spare time I'm developing a JRPG inspired by Norse mythology in Unity, where I get to combine programming, 3D, game design and storytelling.
